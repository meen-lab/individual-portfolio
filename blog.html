SISWAGO: Student E-Hailing Application

University students frequently face challenges in commuting between campuses and surrounding areas due to existing ride-hailing services being prohibitively expensive or lacking adequate safety measures.

To address these gaps, this study introduces SISWAGO, a specialized ride-hailing platform designed specifically for the university ecosystem. SISWAGO prioritizes student-friendly pricing structures and enhanced security protocols to ensure a reliable transportation alternative.

To optimize service quality and driver selection, the platform utilizes a Hybrid Multi-Criteria Decision-Making (MCDM) approach, integrating the Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP) for weighting key evaluation criteria with the Technique for Order of Preference by Similarity to Ideal Solution (TOPSIS) for ranking alternatives.
